MarginMark privacy policy

Last updated October 7, 2026

This policy explains what information the MarginMark Chrome extension and account service collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. MarginMark is made by Plainsman Software ("we", "us"). MarginMark is not affiliated with or endorsed by TikTok.

1. The short version

  • MarginMark reads TikTok Shop Seller Center pages only when you open them, to calculate profit on your products.
  • Your products, costs, profit results and imported files stay on your computer. They are never sent to us.
  • Our servers keep only what's needed to run your account and billing.
  • We don't sell your data and we don't use it for advertising.
  • You can delete your account at any time from inside the extension.

2. What we collect

InformationWhy
Account details: your email address, an optional name, and your password stored only as a secure hash. If you sign in with Google, Facebook or TikTok, we store that provider's account ID for you.To create your account, verify your email, sign you in and contact you about your account.
Plan and billing status: your plan, trial, renewal and cancellation dates, any scheduled plan change, and your Stripe customer and subscription IDs.To know which features you've paid for. Stripe handles your card. We never see or store your full card number.
Trial record: a one-way code made from your email address and, when Stripe provides it, a one-way code made from your card's fingerprint. Neither can be turned back into your email or card.To give each person one free trial, including after an account is deleted.
Support messages: your email address, your message and, if you choose, page details (extension version, page type and whether a price was read).To answer you and fix problems. Page details never include your costs or profit.
Usage events: the names of features you use and coarse counts, with your account ID. They exclude your email, web addresses, product titles, statement rows and cost data.To keep MarginMark working and decide what to improve.
Server logs: IP address, request time and errors.To keep the service secure and fix outages.

MarginMark doesn't collect your TikTok password or session cookies, your browsing outside Seller Center, your buyers' personal information, or the contents of your Seller Center messages. On Seller Center pages it never reads your name, email, phone number or address.

3. What stays on your computer

These are stored in Chrome's extension storage on your computer and are never uploaded to us:

  • products read from Seller Center pages (names, product IDs, prices and promotion prices);
  • your product costs, settings and profit results;
  • settlement statements, creator (affiliate) order files and ad-spend files you import;
  • creator sample logs and the monthly P&L;
  • weekly recap snapshots (the last 12 weeks);
  • your shop's account health rating, its metric cards and your Shop Performance Score.

Uninstalling the extension or clearing its data removes them.

4. How we use information

We use it only to provide, secure, support and improve MarginMark; to process payments and send account and billing emails, such as sign-in codes, receipts and a reminder before a free trial ends; and to meet legal obligations. We don't send marketing emails unless you've agreed to receive them, and every one includes a way to stop.

5. Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent personal information. We share it only with service providers that run parts of MarginMark for us, under contracts that limit how they use it:

  • Stripe, for payments and subscriptions;
  • Fly.io, for application hosting;
  • Neon, for our database;
  • Google Workspace, for sending and receiving email;
  • New Relic, for operational monitoring.

If you sign in with Google, Facebook or TikTok, that provider receives the sign-in request. We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect people's safety or our rights, or as part of a sale or merger of the business. If that happens, this policy will continue to apply.

6. Chrome Web Store limited use

MarginMark's use of information received from Chrome APIs follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use this data only to provide and improve MarginMark's single purpose: showing TikTok Shop sellers the profit on their products. We don't transfer it to others except as described in this policy, we don't use it for advertising, and we don't use it to determine creditworthiness or for lending.

7. How long we keep it

  • We keep account information while your account is open.
  • When you delete your account, we cancel any subscription, remove your saved payment methods from Stripe, and delete your account, sign-in details, plan record and settings right away. Your account ID is removed from usage events.
  • Billing records stay with Stripe, marked as deleted, for as long as tax law requires, which is usually seven years.
  • The trial record (the one-way codes above) is kept after deletion so a free trial can't be repeated.
  • Support messages are deleted from our database after two years. When you delete your account, messages older than 30 days are deleted right away, and the rest lose your email address and are deleted after 30 days.
  • Server logs are kept for up to 90 days. Database backups expire within 30 days.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Change your email or password, or sign out of every device, from the account screen in MarginMark.
  • Delete your account from the account screen, or email privacy@plainsmansoftware.com.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, or for us to correct it, at privacy@plainsmansoftware.com.

Depending on where you live, you may have other rights under laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act or the GDPR. Email us and we'll respond within 30 days. We won't treat you differently for using these rights.

9. Security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS), hashed passwords, restricted access to production systems, and two-factor authentication on our own accounts. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information, we'll notify you as the law requires.

10. Children

MarginMark is for business sellers and isn't directed to anyone under 18. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

11. This website

plainsmansoftware.com uses no cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. Our host may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and pages requested, for security.

12. Changes to this policy

We'll post any changes here and update the date at the top. If a change materially affects how we use your information, we'll email you or show a notice in MarginMark before it takes effect.
