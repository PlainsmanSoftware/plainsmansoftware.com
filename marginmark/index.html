How it works

No spreadsheets to keep in sync. MarginMark reads what Seller Center already shows you.

  1. Open Seller Center

    Open Manage products and click Sync. MarginMark picks up your products and their current prices, including promotions.

  2. Add what each product costs you

    Type a cost per product, or paste a whole column from a spreadsheet using product ID or seller SKU.

  3. See who's losing money

    Every product is marked healthy, thin or losing, with what you keep per sale and the price where it breaks even.

What you see

Profit per sale at a glance

Green, yellow and red for every product, sorted so the biggest leaks are at the top.

The fix for each losing product

The break-even price, plus the highest creator commission and ad spend each product can carry before it loses money.

Profit per creator

Import your affiliate orders and see which creators make you money after commission, fees and cost: keep, renegotiate or drop.

Your real TikTok fees

Import a settlement statement and MarginMark uses the fees TikTok actually charged you instead of an estimate.

What-if price simulator

Try a new price, cost or commission and see what you'd keep before you change anything in Seller Center.

Shop Performance alerts and a weekly recap

A heads-up when your account health moves, and a weekly summary of new losers and the ones you fixed.

Questions

Do I need to connect my TikTok account or give you a password?

No. MarginMark reads the Seller Center pages you open in your own browser. There's no account connection and no TikTok password to share.

Where does my data go?

Imported statements and creator order files stay on your computer, and support messages never include your costs or profit. The privacy policy lists exactly what we store and why.

How does the free trial work?

Pro is free for 7 days. If you cancel before the trial ends, you aren't charged. Otherwise it continues at $14.99 a month, or $120 a year if you picked yearly.

How do I cancel, and can I get a refund?

In MarginMark, open your account and choose Manage billing or cancel.

Cancel anytime from Manage billing. You keep your plan until the end of the period you paid for. Cancel during the free trial and you won't be charged. If you're unhappy with your first charge, email support@plainsmansoftware.com within 7 days of it and we'll refund it in full. Renewals aren't refunded except where the law requires.

Which browsers does it work in?

Google Chrome on a desktop or laptop computer.

Is MarginMark made by TikTok?

No. MarginMark is made by Plainsman Software, an independent company, and is not affiliated with or endorsed by TikTok.
